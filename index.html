Pixel Wings – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Pixel Wings ("the game") uses, and why. The game is made by an independent developer ("we").

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no online servers of our own. Your progress (score, coins, unlocked birds, pipes and worlds, settings and missions) is saved only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or use Reset Data in the settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads (for example, to revive or to double your coins) using Google AdMob, a service of Google. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as:

  • the device advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location,
  • device type, operating system and app usage information related to the ads,
  • cookies or similar identifiers.

This data is processed by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from the PRIVACY button in the game's Settings.

Your choices (United States)

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (for example California), you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for advertising at any time from the PRIVACY button in the game's Settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
